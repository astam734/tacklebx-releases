AI-powered teaching infrastructure for professors. Runs entirely on your Mac (Apple silicon), on your Claude subscription.

Download the latest DMG

Install

  1. Open the DMG and drag Tacklebx to Applications.
  2. If an older Tacklebx is installed, replace it when asked.
  3. First launch: right-click the app and choose Open.

The app checks this page's update feed and tells you when a newer version exists — it never downloads or installs updates by itself.
