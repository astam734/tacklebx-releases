What changed in each posted version of the series on superintelligence and conflict

Allan C. Stam · University of Virginia

These are working papers and they change. Each paper keeps one address that always holds its latest version, and the first page of the technical paper and of its plain-language guide carries the version number and date. A paper and its guide share a number and are replaced together. A new number means a claim, a condition, a result or a source changed; small corrections of wording are made without one. Earlier versions are kept and linked here.
